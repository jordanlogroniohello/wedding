Our Story

How it all started

Nagsimula lahat nung college days — two students na nag-overnight for thesis with multiple groups. Hindi naman kami magka-group, pero fate had other plans.

I still vividly remember that morning. Tapos na ang overnight, pagod na lahat, at hinahanap na ng kanya-kanyang magulang. Si Angelica, mataray talaga — she said "Una na ko, uuwi na ko."

So sabi ko...

"WAIT, may nakalimutan ka."

Naka-taas ang kilay niya. "Ano yon?"

"Naiwan mo ko."

She smiled. She tried to hide it — pero kita ko. Tumawa siya, ngumiti, at that moment... I just knew.

That was 2016. Ten years, countless adventures, and one big question later — here we are. The rest is history.

Since November 6, 2016
